      #HavingFilter

      ExempleDescriptionLibelle
      add-having-filterRegrouper par region puis filtrer les regions performantes avec des ventes superieures a un million-
      add-multiple-having-filterAjouter plusieurs conditions Having en chaine pour filtrer les regions avec ventes et profits eleves-
      clear-having-filterEffacer toutes les conditions Having et afficher le resultat complet de l agregation groupee-
      having-array-value-with-in-operatorHaving filter with array value that triggers 'in' operator conversion-
      having-array-value-with-not-in-operatorHaving filter with array value that triggers 'not in' operator conversion-
      having-clear-and-rebuildClear existing having conditions and rebuild new grouped filters, simulating a user resetting and reconfiguring the filter panel-
      having-deeply-nested-groupsThree-level nested grouping: OR(AND(sales > 500K, profit > 50K), AND(quantity > 100, average discount < 0.3)), simulating complex business filtering-
      having-empty-dsl-compose-targetStart from an empty DSL and use builder to assemble where, having, measures, and dimensions, including a having condition combination with sum and countDistinct aggregations-
      having-field-not-in-measures-and-dimensionsInitialiser une DSL vide, ajouter seulement la dimension area et la mesure sales via builder, puis utiliser dans having le champ profit absent de measures/dimensions (SUM(profit) > 100000)-
      having-find-and-updateAdd having conditions first, then locate them with find and dynamically update thresholds and operators to simulate interactive filter adjustment-
      having-group-add-to-existingAppend a new condition to an existing having group, simulating progressive filter refinement-
      having-group-remove-conditionRemove a specific condition from an existing having group, simulating a user canceling a filter item-
      having-mix-filters-and-groupsMix standalone filter conditions and an OR group: sales > 500K AND (profit > 100K OR quantity >= 30)-
      having-multi-dimension-aggregateGroup by category and region, filter combinations with average discount below 20% and total sales over 100K, and analyze high-value low-discount business-
      having-nested-groupsNested grouping: AND(sales > 1M, OR(profit > 200K, quantity >= 50))-
      having-or-groupUse an OR group to filter regions with high sales or high profit-
      having-scatter-profit-analysisScatter chart analysis: group by category and filter categories with high profit ratio and more than 20 transactions to identify high-quality business-
      having-update-group-operatorUpdate an existing Having group operator from AND to OR-
      having-with-where-combinedCombined where and having filtering: first use where to filter Office Supplies, then use having to filter provinces with sales over 50K or profit over 10K-
      remove-having-filterRemove redundant Having filters and keep only the profit filter-

      #WhereFilter

      ExempleDescriptionLibelle
      between-sales-range-analysisSales range analysis: use between to filter orders from 1000 to 10000 and summarize profit by category-
      clear-and-rebuild-filtersClear and rebuild filters: clear old simple filters and rebuild complex grouped conditions-
      date-filter-period-and-range-comboDate-period and range filtering: use period to filter 2024 Q1 data while range limits the profit interval, then cross-analyze by category and ship mode-
      date-filter-relative-with-nested-conditionsDate filtering with nested conditions: filter high-value orders from Consumer or Corporate customers in the last 30 days and summarize sales and profit by province-
      deeply-nested-or-and-groupsMulti-level nested grouping: high-value orders with Same Day shipping for Consumer customers or First Class shipping for Corporate customers, using three levels of AND/OR nesting-
      high-discount-tech-profit-analysisHigh-discount Technology product profit analysis: filter Technology orders with discount greater than 0.5 and compare profit by region-
      in-operator-multi-area-deliveryMulti-region delivery efficiency comparison: use in to filter East China, North China, and Central South, then count orders by ship mode-
      nested-group-region-product-filterOffice Supplies or Furniture sales in East China: use nested grouping with AND connecting the region condition and OR category conditions-
      not-between-sales-rangeNot between filter: exclude sales between 1000~10000-
      not-between-with-explicit-operatorsNot between filter with explicit leftOp/rightOp to test invert functions-
      office-supplies-sales-by-provinceOffice Supplies sales ranking by province: filter the Office Supplies category and summarize sales by province-
      or-group-product-category-comparisonOffice Supplies versus Technology category comparison: use an OR group to filter two categories and compare sales by region-
      remove-condition-from-groupRemove a condition from a group: the preset OR group contains three categories, and updateGroup removes one of them-
      remove-filter-by-indexRemove a filter by index: remove the first category filter and keep only the region condition-
      update-filter-switch-provinceDynamically modify a filter: update the province filter from Zhejiang to Guangdong and observe sales changes-
      update-group-or-to-andModify group logic: switch the preset OR category group to AND to narrow the filter scope-
      where-filter-array-value-converts-to-inWhere filter with array value using '=' operator should convert to 'in'-
      where-filter-array-value-converts-to-not-inWhere filter with array value using '!=' operator should convert to 'not in'-
